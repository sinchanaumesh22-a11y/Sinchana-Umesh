Our Philosophy

More than a cup.
An intentional ritual.

Founded on the belief that coffee is culinary poetry, AURA is an antidote to rushed mornings. Here, water temperature is calibrated to the exact degree, milk is textured into silk, and every bean has a name, an altitude, and an origin family.

Whether you are settling into a velvet armchair with an Ethiopian pour-over, pairing a warm pistachio croissant with our velvet Spanish latte, or gathering around our community roaster table, AURA was built to be your third place—between work and home.
